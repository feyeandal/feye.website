Hi, I'm Feye!

I am a geospatial analyst and project manager based in [Quezon City, Philippines](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/106569). I focus on helping purpose-driven organizations to creating powerful and social impacts through open data and free and open-source software (FOSS).

I specialize in automating geospatial data processing and analysis workflows using Python, ensuring reproducibility and scalability (_and avoiding repetitive tasks!_). I am passionate about creating compelling data visualizations, including maps, graphs, and posters, using FOSS.

I provide consultancy services for organizations such as the Asian Development Bank and the World Bank, focusing on geospatial data science workflows and data collection. Additionally, I manage and supervise the [UP Resilience Institute YouthMappers](https://www.facebook.com/UPRIYouthMappers) team, supporting its mission to empower local communities and strengthen disaster resilience through open mapping. I previously served as a [YouthMappers](https://youthmappers.org) Regional Ambassador from 2020 to 2024 and continue to contribute to [OpenStreetMap-Philippines](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Philippines) and promote open mapping initiatives.

Beyond working with geospatial datasets and handling various WebGIS projects, I enjoy cooking, traveling, watching series and movies, reading books, taking photos, and designing.
